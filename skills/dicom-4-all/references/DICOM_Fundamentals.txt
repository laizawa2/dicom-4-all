1. DICOM FUNDAMENTALS
- DICOM (Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine) is the worldwide standard for medical imaging interoperability.
- Developed from the ACR-NEMA standard.
- There is no DICOM 4.0. The term DICOM 3.0 is historical.
- DICOM provides interconnectivity, not guaranteed interoperability.
- Interoperability depends on matching SOP Classes, Transfer Syntaxes, roles, workflows, and data requirements.
- DICOM works in parallel with HL7 and healthcare integration initiatives.

COMMENTS OF NOTE:
- Detailed explanation of ACR, NEMA and DICOM workgroups.
- WADO explained as web-based DICOM retrieval using HTTP.
- MIME types for DICOM files defined as application/dicom.
